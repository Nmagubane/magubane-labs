Our product

Bonisa

by Magubane Labs

Bonisa means show in isiZulu. In maths you show your working. In science you show why.

Maths and science for South African learners from Grade 1 to Grade 12, organised by grade and topic, with a read-aloud experience for Grades 1 to 3 and a mode for teachers. Learners don’t just get marked: they find out what went wrong and how to fix it.

Pilot, by invitation Ask about the pilot Sign in

Only 34 in 100 matric candidates wrote Mathematics in 2025.

Most wrote Mathematical Literacy instead (Department of Basic Education). Learners choose at the end of Grade 9, but the confidence behind that choice is built or lost from the early grades. Bonisa works on that confidence, one skill at a time.

  • Practice at the right levelQuestions adjust to each learner, so the work stays hard enough to learn from and possible to get right.
  • Mistakes explained, not just markedA wrong answer is matched to the misunderstanding behind it, with a tip to fix it.
  • A tutor that teachesFor Grades 4 to 12, the AI tutor asks and hints before it tells. It is switched off during tests.
  • Tests that measureTimed, with no hints, and different numbers for every learner, so a result means something.
  • Built for teachersClasses, assignments and a clear view of what each learner needs next.
  • Grades 1 to 3, read aloudChildren sign in with pictures on a device an adult has set up. No points, streaks or stars.

How we work

Four rules we don’t bend.

  • Start with a real problem

    We measure the problem before we build, and judge our work by whether it moved.

  • Fewer things, done well

    One feature that works beats ten that half work.

  • Built for South Africa

    Phones from 320 pixels wide, data that costs money, and POPIA from the first line of code.

  • Nothing pretends

    If something isn’t built yet, we say so. No screen shows a feature that doesn’t work.
