Bonisa

by Magubane Labs

Bonisa means “show” in isiZulu. In maths you show your working. In science you show why.

A maths and science platform for South African learners from Grade 1 to Grade 12, organised by grade and topic, with a separate read-aloud experience for Grades 1 to 3 and a mode for teachers.

In the 2025 matric exams, only 34% of candidates wrote Mathematics; most wrote Mathematical Literacy (Department of Basic Education). Learners choose at the end of Grade 9, but the confidence behind that choice is built or lost from the early grades. Bonisa works on that confidence, one skill at a time.
